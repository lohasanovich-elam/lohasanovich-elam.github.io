LUMINESCENCE

Project Team Scale: 2 Programmers + 2D Artist

Project Role: Programmer

Development Time: 3.5 Months

Project Runtime: February 2026 - June 2026

SYNOPSIS:

'LUMINESCENCE' is a 2D-Platformer where the emotionally struggling character, 'Lumi', enters a mysterious cave that allows them to reflect on their past. This cave allows for them to harness their emotions as a key to their movement, depending on how they feel, with the gimmick of the game being that the character fluctuates through different emotions that impact your movement throughout the cave. This game was made in the aspiriation of conveying a 'social justice message', to which our team agreed to tackle mental health in-terms of 'bipolar disorder'. We wanted to make sure we could represent this in a way that wouldn't strike as harmful like media representation.

'LUMINESCENCE' has the gameplay make it so that your main character will slowly get more sadder without much movement, this will cause them to dramatically get slower until they reach a state that makes them incredibly slow. These work by having 5 states of emotions which range from 'Depressed' to 'Ecstatic', both are opposites that make the game a lot harder to control so you would be encouraged to keep 'Lumi' at a middleground. There are also various items spread across levels that affect their moveset, including extra dashes or a bit of control to the moodswings mechanic, which will randomly fluctuate their current state of emotion.

This was one of my largest coding projects by far so everyone on the team was able to communicate ideas well to eachother in our time together and were able to make a game that we are proud of. There were some creative hurdles, with the artist being the only one on the team but me and the other programmer were able to give input and evolve the game accordingly.
